<objective>
Upgrade the AR0144 stereo camera's capture resolution in `control/vla_bridge/stereo_camera.py` from the just-implemented 1280x360 (640x360 per half) to 1600x600 (800x600 per half) -- a meaningfully higher-resolution mode confirmed live and stable via raw ffmpeg testing (20 consecutive frames, all showing genuine non-zero frame-to-frame pixel differences ~4.9, no freezing) and visually confirmed via a saved test frame to be the correct side-by-side stereo layout with visible parallax between the two halves. Propagate the resolution change and its full rationale through every dependent doc/comment/test that describes it, without touching any code that already derives dimensions dynamically at runtime.

Purpose: give the VLA bridge camera feed roughly 2x more pixels per eye than the prior 1280x360 fix, while staying on the confirmed-stable side of this hardware's mode list -- the still-broken 2560x720 mode remains frozen even after exhausting the last known macOS system-level fix (legacy-camera-plugins override + fresh reboot), and the only other candidate modes (1280x480, 1280x712) turned out to be single-lens crops, not usable stereo pairs. 1600x600 is therefore the best available resolution on this hardware.
Output: `stereo_camera.py` with the new constants and an updated docstring documenting the full resolution history/rationale; `test_stereo_camera.py`'s dimension assertions updated to match; `policy_server_launch.md`'s Camera mapping section, crop-column table, and Step 5 launch command updated to the new crop; `stereo_calibration.py`/`depth_camera.py`/their tests re-verified (not just assumed) to need zero changes; full `control/` test suite passing.
</objective>
