**Physical derivation (read before editing, so the fix is applied correctly, not just "sign negated"):**

In `soarm_gripper.xml`, at `qpos=0` a slide joint always sits at its body's nominal (undisplaced) position — that is a geometric fact independent of axis sign. Today `gripper_left`'s nominal geom offset (`pos="-0.052 -0.0092 0"` on `left_jaw_collision`) and `gripper_right`'s (`pos="-0.052 0.0088 0"` on `right_jaw_collision`) sit close together (edges touching at the "grip line" y=-0.0002) — so `qpos=(0,0)` is always physically CLOSED, and this does not change no matter which way the axis points. What the axis sign controls is only which direction each jaw travels as qpos moves away from 0. Today: `gripper_left axis="0 -1 0"`, `gripper_right axis="0 1 0"`, `range="0 0.042"` on both — increasing qpos moves each jaw AWAY from the other (open), so `qpos=(0.042,0.042)` = OPEN (84mm apart), matching the actuator comment `current_action=-1 => ctrl 0 (closed), +1 => ctrl 0.042 (open)`.

A bare axis-sign negation on both joints (`0 -1 0`->`0 1 0` and `0 1 0`->`0 -1 0`) with the range left at `0 0.042` would make increasing qpos move the jaws TOWARD each other and then PAST each other (physically invalid — they'd try to occupy the same space, which the box-collision contacts would fight against, producing broken/jammed behavior, not a clean open state). To reverse the closed/open polarity while preserving the exact same physical geometry (same touching-closed and 84mm-apart-open positions, still mirrored, still non-crossing), the axis flip must be paired with negating+reordering the joint's `range` from `0 0.042` to `-0.042 0` (and the matching `<position>` actuator's `ctrlrange` from `0 0.042` to `-0.042 0`, for both `gripper_left` and `gripper_right`). Under this paired change: `qpos=(0,0)` is still CLOSED (unchanged, as established above), but `qpos=(-0.042,-0.042)` now reaches the exact same 84mm-apart OPEN position as `qpos=(0.042,0.042)` used to. `ctrl=high(0)` still maps to CLOSED; `ctrl=low(-0.042)` now maps to OPEN — the reverse of today's `ctrl=low(0)=closed, ctrl=high(0.042)=open`. Since robosuite's `current_action`(-1..1)->`ctrl` rescale always maps `current_action=-1` to `ctrl=low` and `current_action=+1` to `ctrl=high`, this makes `current_action=-1` now mean OPEN (was CLOSED) and `current_action=+1` now mean CLOSED (was OPEN) — the reversal TWIN-07 needs, achieved without moving any geom/body position and without touching `SoarmGripper.format_action`'s arithmetic.

Treat the specific numeric targets above (`-0.042 0` for both ranges/ctrlranges) as the derived, expected-correct values, but the actual acceptance bar is the empirical check in Task 1's `<verify>` (monotonic, non-crossing 84mm separation) — if that check fails, the range/ctrlrange bounds are wrong and must be adjusted until it passes, not the derivation logic.

Grepped confirmation (informs Task 3's STATE.md note): `LIBERO/libero/libero/datasets/collector.py` lines 81-82 hardcode `OPEN_CMD = -1.0` / `CLOSE_CMD = 1.0` for its Phase-4-era scripted grasp FSM, relying on TODAY's (pre-fix) polarity. After this fix, those constants will drive the physical opposite of their names. This file is NOT touched by this plan (Phase 8/9 demo re-collection that uses it is currently paused) — it is only documented as a new concern in Task 3.
