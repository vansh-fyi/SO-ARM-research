<objective>
Integrate RDT-1B (VLAB-09) as a Tier C backend, reusing Plan 14-03's proven `fastfs_server.py`-derived adapter shape. 14-RESEARCH.md characterizes RDT-1B's action space as "a unified action space with a joint-position slot usable directly for SO-101's 6 DOF" -- this plan's quality gate requires that characterization be CONFIRMED against the real checkpoint's config (not silently assumed), with `eef_to_joint.solve_ik()` used as a fallback if it turns out otherwise.
