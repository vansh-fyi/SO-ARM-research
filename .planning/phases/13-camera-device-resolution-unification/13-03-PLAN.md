<objective>
Wire Plan 13-01's shared `resolve_camera_identifier()` helper into the ONE path that actually drives real research episodes: `run_vla_episode.py --server-address` (the live Colab-bridged SmolVLA path). This is the second independently-verifiable unit of the phase -- with 13-01 landed, this plan makes the live inference-input AND IOLogger-recording paths both consume the SAME name-resolved camera identifiers, closing CAMFIX-01 for the production path, and carries the phase's mandatory CAMFIX-02 live re-verification human-check. Per D-05, that re-verification must be a FRESH live episode -- not a re-citation of Phase 12's evidence -- because this phase adds a new by-name resolution layer on top of the already-working Phase 12 mechanism. Per D-06, the re-verification's pass criteria must cover BOTH the overhead AND wrist frames, since wrist resolution is newly name-based this phase.

Purpose: Phase 12's Gap 1 closure already proved the "one shared StereoSplitCamera instance" mechanism works for the overhead camera; this plan is what makes the IDENTIFIER feeding that mechanism (and the wrist camera's identifier) come from a name-based resolution instead of a raw, potentially-stale device_map.json index.
Output: `run_vla_episode.py`'s device_map-defaulted camera identifiers routed through `detect_devices.resolve_camera_identifier()`; `control/vla_bridge/robot_client.py` unchanged (confirmed via its existing test suite staying green).

Out of scope per D-02: `run_vla_episode.py`'s non-bridge `ScriptedActionSource` path (no `--server-address`) is deliberately left untouched by this plan -- it is a throwaway dry-run harness that never consumes camera frames (no policy, no vision), so the original "two independent opens of one device" collision cannot occur there, and extending name-based resolution into it would be scope creep into pure dev/test scaffolding.
</objective>
