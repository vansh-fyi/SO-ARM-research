<objective>
Build the foundation this phase's two consumer-wiring plans (13-03, 13-04) depend on: a single shared, name-based camera-resolution helper usable by BOTH the wrist and stereo-overhead cameras (CAMFIX-01's literal "single shared, name-based camera-resolution helper" requirement), plus the one-line gap that currently prevents the wrist camera from ever being resolved by name at all -- `resolve_cameras()` already computes the wrist camera's real device name via `_correlate_names()` but discards it before returning.

This is the smallest complete, independently-verifiable unit of the phase: fully unit-testable in isolation, with zero dependency on the two live consumer paths (`run_vla_episode.py`, `record_episode.py`) that will call it next wave.

Purpose: Without this, Plan 13-03 and 13-04 have no shared function to wire into -- they would each have to reinvent name-first/index-fallback resolution independently, defeating CAMFIX-01's "single shared helper" requirement.
Output: `resolve_camera_identifier(role, device_map, correlate_fn=None) -> int | str` and its supporting `_live_correlate_names()`, both in `control/detect_devices.py`, fully unit-tested.
</objective>
