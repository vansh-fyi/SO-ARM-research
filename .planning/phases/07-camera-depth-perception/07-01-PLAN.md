<objective>
Recalibrate the sim `agentview` (overhead) and `eye_in_hand` (wrist) cameras so their pose/FOV are grounded in the real SOARM camera hardware instead of values inherited unmodified from the Panda-based LIBERO benchmark (CAM-01, plus the eye_in_hand scope addition from 07-CONTEXT.md D-06/D-07).

Per D-01, the real overhead camera's mount does not exist yet, so `agentview`'s pos/quat/fovy are a documented best-estimate (AR0144 datasheet FOV + a reasonable overhead-workspace placement), not a value blocked on physical hardware completion. The chosen pose approximates D-02's real mount style (a fixed frame/arm clamped near the desk/table edge, not a distant tripod) and D-03's compact custom-cradle-housed AR0144 body — the physical cradle/clamp fabrication itself stays out of this phase's software scope. Per D-04, the literal real-camera-vs-sim-render side-by-side photo comparison is explicitly deferred until the physical overhead mount is built (tracked as a follow-up outside this plan); this plan's `agentview` acceptance bar is a documented, non-degenerate, real-spec-derived recalibration, not a live photo match.

Purpose: Close the sim-to-real view gap for the two cameras that feed both scripted data collection and VLA inference, so recorded/inferred frames resemble what the physical AR0144 (overhead) and IMX335 (wrist) cameras will actually see.
Output: `agentview` renders with a real-camera-derived 43 deg vertical FOV and a lower/closer desk-mount-appropriate pose; `eye_in_hand` renders tilted toward the gripper's grasp point; an automated fovy regression test; a human sign-off comparing `eye_in_hand`'s new framing against the 8 real reference photos.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** researcher building the SoARM VLA data/fine-tuning pipeline, **I want to** the simulated `agentview` and `eye_in_hand` cameras to match the real SOARM camera mounts' placement and angle, **so that** frames recorded in sim (and later used for fine-tuning) resemble what the physical cameras will actually capture, closing the sim-to-real perception gap.

This plan is the camera-recalibration slice of that goal — after it lands, a researcher can render either camera and see a pose/FOV grounded in real hardware specs/photos rather than an unmodified Panda-benchmark default. It is independent of the depth-persistence slices (07-02, 07-03): no shared files, can execute in the same wave.
